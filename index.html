02 // why it can squeeze

  • Every share sold short must be bought back. 54.7M shares of forced future demand already exist.
  • Borrow gets expensive when lendable shares get scarce. Shares held and never lent tighten that supply.
  • Small float means a treasury of tens of millions is a real fraction of the stock, not a rounding error.
  • The sector trades on narrative. Narrative is what a coordination game produces.

03 // the mechanism

Every OP829 trade pays a 5% tax. 3% of every trade goes to the Vault. The Vault accumulates QUBT exposure on a published schedule, in slow scheduled tranches, and does not sell. A buy feeds it. A sell feeds it. Panic feeds it.

The game exists to keep the trades coming after launch week. Seasons follow the short-interest calendar. Missions reward holding through tranche days. Squads compete for sectors of the float.
